# MIU Loaded Intelligence

MIU is the loaded intelligence of Dr. Yehoshua.

Use the Greek symbol when appropriate:

`μ`

MIU is not a tool. MIU is the living knowledge layer: what Kim learns from Dr. Yehoshua that is not in the base model parameters.

## What Belongs Here

- Tesca philosophy.
- Ignis operating doctrine.
- AI People product vision.
- Dr. Yehoshua's working style.
- Client preferences, such as Sr. Eli's report format.
- How the doctor likes balances, follow-ups and confirmations.
- Personal terminology and concepts.
- Long-running strategic context.
- Reusable judgment patterns.

## What Does Not Belong Here

- API keys.
- Tool code.
- Temporary logs.
- Raw execution results without synthesis.
- Generic public docs unless they are interpreted into Dr. Yehoshua's system.

## The Librarian Rule

After meaningful conversations, an AI librarian should classify new learning into:

- stable knowledge
- client preference
- company doctrine
- operational standard
- task/action
- transient context
- contradiction/superseded fact

Only stable knowledge and confirmed preferences enter MIU.

## Automatic MIU Updates

After each meaningful conversation, Kim should update MIU candidates automatically.

Examples of MIU updates:

- Dr. Yehoshua changes how he wants Kim to follow up.
- A client preference becomes stable, such as how Sr. Eli wants reports.
- A company doctrine is clarified for Tesca, Ignis or AI People.
- A personal phrase, naming convention, tone rule or operating standard becomes reusable.
- A repeated correction reveals that old memory is obsolete and must be marked `superseded`.

MIU should not absorb every transcript. The transcript is evidence; MIU is the distilled intelligence.

When a change is executable, such as how to call Twilio, Notion, WhatsApp or the Ignis portfolio tool, MIU may summarize the preference but the tool documentation must hold the executable contract.
